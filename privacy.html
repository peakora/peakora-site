Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy explains how Peakora collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to keeping your data safe and treating it with respect. By using Peakora, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us provide a better experience. This includes information you choose to share and information collected automatically when you use our services.

  • Information you enter in forms, such as your email or messages.
  • Basic device and browser information.
  • Usage data that helps us understand how people use Peakora.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to improve Peakora, communicate with you, and provide a smoother experience.

  • To respond to your messages or requests.
  • To improve our tools and features.
  • To understand how users interact with Peakora.
  • To maintain the safety and stability of our services.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We use security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. While no system is perfect, we work to keep your data as safe as possible.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Peakora may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your experience. These tools help us remember your preferences and understand how people use our website.

5. Third Party Services & Payment Processing

Peakora integrates with trusted third-party service providers to deliver our features. Specifically, when you purchase a Peakora+ subscription, payment processing and order management are handled securely by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. Dodo Payments collects transaction details necessary to process payment, issue receipts, and manage tax compliance. Peakora does not store full payment card numbers or banking information on our servers.

6. Data Retention

Your personal reflection notes, check-ins, and 7-day plans are stored locally in your browser's client-side storage. We retain account-level membership flags only as long as necessary to provide subscription access or fulfill legal obligations. You can clear your local data at any time via your browser settings or by resetting your plan within the Peakora Assistant.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, export, or request permanent deletion of any personal information we hold. To exercise your data privacy rights, please contact our Privacy Team at peakora.network@gmail.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

Peakora is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that information was collected from a child, we will remove it.

9. International Users

If you access Peakora from outside your home country, your information may be processed in other regions. By using Peakora, you agree to this processing.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of Peakora means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or your personal information, please email our Privacy Officer at peakora.network@gmail.com. We respond to all privacy inquiries within 24–48 hours (Monday through Friday).

Frequently Asked Questions

How and when do I get paid?

You choose your payout method, PayPal, Wise, bank transfer, or USDC. Payouts are issued monthly once your balance reaches a $25 minimum. To protect against chargebacks and refunds, each commission is held for 30 days before it is eligible for payout. Request a payout from your portal and our team fulfills it on your schedule.

What counts as a qualifying referral?

A qualifying referral is someone who clicks your unique affiliate link and subscribes to Peakora+ (monthly or annual plan) within the cookie window. Free plan signups do not generate commission until they upgrade to a paid plan.

How long does the cookie last?

Every partner gets a 90-day cookie window. If someone clicks your link and subscribes within that period, you receive the commission, even if they browsed other pages in between.

Is there a minimum audience size to apply?

No. We welcome partners of all sizes, from niche bloggers with engaged communities to large creators. What matters is the quality of your audience and your genuine alignment with wellness and mindful living values.

Do I earn commission on both monthly and annual plans?

Yes. You earn your commission rate on every payment, whether the subscriber chooses the monthly ($9.99/mo) or annual ($95.88/yr) plan. Annual plan referrals generate a larger one-time commission per payment.

What happens if a referral cancels?

If a referred subscriber cancels their Peakora+ subscription, your recurring commission for that referral stops. There are no chargebacks or clawbacks for commissions already paid. You only earn while they remain subscribed.

Can I use my affiliate link in paid ads?

Affiliate links may not be used in paid search or social ads bidding on Peakora-branded keywords. Organic content, blog posts, social media, email newsletters, and YouTube are all encouraged. Full guidelines are provided in your portal.

How do you track my referrals?

When someone clicks your link, a first-party cookie and local storage record your referral code for 90 days. The cookie spans our marketing site and app subdomain. Crucially, commission is not granted by the click, it is computed server-side only when a verified payment webhook from our payment processor confirms a real subscription. This means tracking is tamper-proof: credit is tied to an actual paid transaction, never to a client-side flag. Self-referrals are blocked automatically.

Install Peakora Assistant App

Setup instructions for fast 1-tap launcher access on all your devices:

iPhone & iPad (Safari)
1. Open Peakora in Safari
2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen
3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen"
Android Phones & Tablets (Chrome)
1. Open Peakora in Chrome
2. Tap the Menu (three vertical dots) in top right
3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
Desktop (Mac, Windows, Chromebook)
1. Open in Chrome, Edge, or Safari
2. Click the Install Icon in the browser address bar
3. Or click "Install App Now" below

Refund & Guarantee Policy

7-Day Risk-Free Guarantee & Refund Terms

At Peakora, we are dedicated to helping you build daily calm, focus, and emotional momentum. We want you to feel completely confident in your Peakora+ membership. This Refund & Guarantee Policy outlines our money-back guarantee, cancellation process, and how refunds are handled in partnership with our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments.

1. 7-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee

Every new Peakora+ subscription ($9.99/month or $95.88/year) comes with a 100% 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with your Peakora+ experience for any reason within the first 7 days following your initial purchase, you are eligible for a full, hassle-free refund—no questions asked.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within your 7-day guarantee window, simply contact our support team:

  • Email: Send an email to peakora.network@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request".
  • Include Order Details: Please provide your order email address or transaction ID (received in your receipt from Dodo Payments).
  • Fast Response: Our support team will review and acknowledge your request within 24 to 48 hours.

3. Processing & Refund Timelines

Once your refund request is approved, the order refund is issued immediately through our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. Depending on your financial institution or card issuer, funds will appear back on your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

4. Subscription Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your Peakora+ recurring subscription auto-renewal at any time without fees or penalties.

  • How to Cancel: You can cancel via your account settings within Peakora or by emailing peakora.network@gmail.com.
  • Access After Cancellation: Upon cancellation, your auto-renewal is immediately stopped, but you retain full access to all Peakora+ features until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No Partial Period Proration: Except within the initial 7-day money-back guarantee window, cancellations do not yield partial pro-rated refunds for unused portions of an ongoing billing cycle.

5. Merchant of Record Statement

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller and Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. Dodo Payments is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Dodo Payments provides all customer service inquiries regarding order billing, sales tax compliance, payment issues, and return processing.

6. Contact Support

If you have questions about billing, order charges, or this Refund Policy, please reach out to us at peakora.network@gmail.com. We are here to support your journey every step of the way.
